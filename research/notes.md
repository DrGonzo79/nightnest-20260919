# Source notes

- Public source: [A stargazing setup service for rural Airbnb hosts](https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/a-stargazing-setup-service-for-rural-airbnb-hosts-64e25990)
- Source date: 2026-09-19. Newsletter title: “Stargazing-in-a-box for Airbnb owners.” The email proposes scoring a property's sky, prescribing equipment and placement, giving guests a nightly QR guide, then building a directory and earning booking referrals.
- Timing signal from the source: DarkSky International reportedly took 17 years to certify its first 100 International Dark Sky Places and about seven years for the next 150. This is directional interest, not proof that hosts will pay.
- The detailed public page was attempted with a normal fetch and returned a Vercel security checkpoint (HTTP 429). Browser fallback redirected to a login page, so paywalled scores, keywords, pricing, offer ladder, difficulty, revenue potential, and GTM details were unavailable and are not invented here.
- Product decisions in this repo are original analysis: start with a paid remote assessment, use auditable rules rather than simulated AI, and validate with a concierge cohort before a directory.
- Newsletter text, recipient metadata, tracking redirects, preference links, and unsubscribe links are intentionally excluded.
